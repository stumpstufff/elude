07 / Data

Privacy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Elude ("the game") collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can access or delete it.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Stumps-Stuff ("we", "us"). Contact: stumpstufff@gmail.com.

What we collect

Voice is not recorded or stored. It is relayed in real time to players in your session and then discarded. Head and hand positions are also relayed in real time and not stored.

We do not collect your real name, email address, payment details, location, camera images, or contacts. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run the game. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Microsoft PlayFab (Microsoft Corporation) stores your account, display name, save data, stats and moderation records. Data is stored in the United States. Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Photon (Exit Games GmbH, Germany) runs multiplayer and voice chat servers and relays your display name, avatar movement and voice to other players. Processing happens in the server region you connect to: the United States (US East, US West and US South Central regions). Photon Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Platforms provides the platform the game runs on and handles purchases. Meta Privacy Policy.

Other players in your session can see your display name and avatar and hear your voice. We may disclose data if required by law.

How long we keep it

  • Account, progress and leaderboard data: until you ask us to delete it, or after 2 years with no sign-in.
  • Moderation records: for as long as the ban lasts, plus up to 1 year.
  • Voice, movement and connection data: not stored beyond the live session.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. Email stumpstufff@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and include your in-game name. We will confirm your request and delete your account and associated data from our systems and our service providers within 30 days. Deleting your data resets your progress.

You can turn off voice chat or mute your microphone in the game's settings, and you can revoke the game's access to your Meta account information in your Meta Horizon account settings.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights, including the right to object to processing and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will delete their data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with our service providers, who use industry-standard security measures. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will be announced in the game.
