FAQ

Things people ask about Elude.

What platforms is Elude on?

Elude is coming to Meta Quest first.

When does it release?

There's no release date yet. Wishlist it on the Meta Quest store to get notified the moment it launches.

How many people can play together?

Elude is built for online co-op with your friends, with proximity voice chat.

Can I join the beta?

Beta testing is coming soon. Meta requires beta testers to be 18 or older. Email me if you're interested.

What age is Elude for?

Elude is aimed at players 13 and up. The scares are real, but the violence is kept stylized.

Is this an official SCP game?

No. Elude is a fan-made game based on the SCP Foundation, used under the CC BY-SA 3.0 license. See the credits for full attribution.

How can I support the game?

Wishlist it, tell your friends, and send feedback. It all helps more than you'd think.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Send it to stumpstufff@gmail.com. I read everything.
