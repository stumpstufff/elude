The game

Containment has failed across the Foundation's underground sites. Elude is a co-op VR horror game where you and your friends are the expendable recovery team sent in to bring back scrap before the anomalies find you.

Every facility is generated fresh. Fight back with guns, melee and grenades, talk over proximity voice chat, sell what you recover, and build up your own outpost between runs.

About me

I'm Stump, and Elude is my first game. I've been building in Unity for about two years, and this is the first project I'm putting out into the world.

I wanted to make the kind of game I'd want to play with my friends: scary, chaotic, and better the more things go wrong.

A wishlist, a share, or a bit of feedback helps me more than you'd think. Thanks for being here early.
